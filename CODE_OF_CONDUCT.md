# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We expect participants to be respectful, constructive, and professional.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or reject contributions that violate this standard. Serious or repeated violations may result in removal from project spaces.

For concerns, open a private communication with a maintainer where the hosting platform provides that facility.
